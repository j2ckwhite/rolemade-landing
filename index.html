How it works

Four steps. You only show up for one.

Most AI advice puts the learning on you. We put it in the tool, and the first one is free.

01Your task

Pick one job

Something you do the same way every week and would rather not. It usually lives in a spreadsheet, an inbox, or a folder of PDFs.

02Your rules

We learn it once

One walkthrough. You show us how you do it, what good looks like, and what to watch for. We write those steps into the tool so you never have to explain them again.

03Your toolkit

You get a tool

A clean page with your company's name on it. Drop in the file, the notes, or the form. The finished work comes out the way you would have done it. Add more tools as you find more jobs.

04Your domain

It lives with you

Your toolkit sits on its own private address. Bookmark it, share it with the person who needs it, open it like any other page. Nothing to install.

Straight answer

Is this for you?

We'd rather tell you now than after a call.

A good fit

  • ✓You or a senior person spends hours a week on work that repeats
  • ✓The task follows the same steps every time
  • ✓You'd rather hand it off than learn a new platform
  • ✓Professional services or similar, roughly 5 to 50 people

Not a fit

  • –The task changes every time it's done
  • –You want a chatbot, or you want to learn prompting yourself
  • –The work needs judgment calls nobody can write down
  • –You're looking for a full software rebuild

The guarantee

  1. Before we build, we pick three real past examples together and agree on what the correct output was.
  2. The tool passes when it reproduces them. Not "feels right." Matches.
  3. If it doesn't pass, there is no build fee. After that it's month to month. Stop any time and the toolkit simply goes offline.
