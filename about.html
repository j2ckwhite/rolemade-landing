Where I've done this

Different industries. Same patterns.

On the ads side: e-commerce in apparel and supplements, home services of every kind, brick-and-mortar stores, and info businesses selling courses and programs online. On the AI side: owners and executives who were doing the same spreadsheet job every week and wanted it off their desk.

After enough accounts, the same few things show up everywhere. The offer matters more than the creative. Tracking gets set up wrong more often than it gets set up right. And the most expensive hours in a business are usually spent on work nobody would miss if it disappeared.

The time-saved numbers I quote come from clients themselves: hours per week they tell me a tool gave back, at what their time is worth. I don't dress those up.

The path

Most of it didn't work.

I started in marketing as a high school freshman, five years ago. Since then I've tried more businesses than I'd like to admit. Most of them failed. Each one taught me something about what actually moves a business forward and what only looks like it does.

The agency is the one that worked. Along the way I've helped dozens of owners grow, seen what held them back, and built the tools I wished existed for my own operation. RoleMade is those two things put together: growth work that's been tested on real accounts, and AI tools that were built for a real business before they were ever sold to one.

That's the whole story. No agency-speak, no team of forty. One person who has done the work and will do it for you.
